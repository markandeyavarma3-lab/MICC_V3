# 0095 — The clean-up of 2026-10-31: what went, what stays, and why

**Date:** 2026-10-31
**Decided by:** Owner asked for the whole project to be checked end to end and
the unwanted files and data deleted. The audit is mine; each deletion below
was chosen by the owner from the audit's list.
**Status:** ACTIVE
**Related:** 0042 (the last clean-up and its protocol), 0027 (the seed),
0093 (backups go to the pen drive only).

## The audit

The protocol of 0042: find each item's use, check what is wired to it,
reconfirm, only then delete. Project size before: 21 GB.

- **The database is clean.** All 19 tables in `research_prod.duckdb` are read
  by code; the two empty ones (`deal_interpretation`, `seasonality_cell`) are
  schema from migrations. Nothing was dropped.
- **Irreplaceable, not offered for deletion:** the seed and increments
  (`data/raw/v1_export`, `v1_increments`), the salvaged predecessor data
  (`data/raw/salvaged`, including the 2 GB seasonality atlas), the raw NSE
  archive (`data/raw/archive`, some of it no longer served), the price and
  F&O spines, the logs.

## Deleted

- **Caches and scratch, 59 MB:** `.pytest_cache`, `.ruff_cache`, every
  `__pycache__` outside `.venv`, `.DS_Store` files, the report build's
  `node_modules` (reinstallable with `npm install` in `docs/report/build`),
  and 17 probe scripts and notes in `.tmp/` that nothing used.
- **The EXPLORE scan, 724 MB:** `data/prod/warehouse/scan/explore_h21_d3`.
  Its manifest is kept at
  `data/derived/deleted_0095/explore_h21_d3.manifest.json`.
  `scripts/scan_confirm.py` reads it, so **run `scripts/scan_explore.py`
  first** before any re-run of the confirmation scan. The registered exp_005
  scan (`confirm_h21_d3`), its 0089 re-measure (`confirm_h21_d3_0088`) and
  exp_006's selection stay as evidence.

## Waiting on the pen drive

- **The old iCloud backups, 14 GB**, outside the project
  (`iCloud Drive/institutional research/backup`), superseded by 0093. They
  are deleted only after the pen drive is mounted and a fresh backup there
  reports GREEN, so there is never a moment without a second copy. On
  2026-10-31 the pen drive was not mounted.

## Kept on reflection

- **About 500 MB of seed tables no code reads** (MICCV2's backtest and paper
  trading tables, option greeks, MF NAV history). Deleting any file changes
  the seed's registered fingerprint: `seed.verify()` and its test would fail,
  both price spines name the seed as their parent, and exp_001 and exp_002
  cite it as their data. The owner chose to keep the seed whole.

## What would reverse this

A need to re-run the confirmation scan (rebuild the EXPLORE scan first), or a
reason to prune the seed after all (register the pruned seed as a new version
with the original as its parent, in a new record).

## Cost accepted

- Re-running `scan_confirm.py` now costs a few hours more.
