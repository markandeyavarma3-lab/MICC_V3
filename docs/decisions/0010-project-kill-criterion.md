# 0010 — The thesis itself can be abandoned

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Decided by:** Owner
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Context
Studies could be killed; the project could not. MICCV2 ran roughly two years,
recorded 22 KILLs and zero promotions, and never once concluded that the approach
did not work.

## Decision
The institutional-behaviour thesis is abandoned, and written up as REJECTED, when
**either**:

- **3 of the 4 studies fail their portfolio gate**, or
- **no study has passed the portfolio gate by 2027-02-28**,

whichever comes first. The deliverable on abandonment is
`docs/reports/FINAL_VERDICT.md` stating plainly that public institutional deal
disclosure does not contain a tradable edge.

## Why
Without a project-level criterion, a project can lose every battle and never
concede the war. A negative result stated clearly is a real deliverable; a project
that quietly fades is not.

## What would reverse this
Only an explicit, dated, written amendment by the owner — not a good week of
results near the deadline. The value of a deadline is entirely in its being
inconvenient when it arrives.

## Cost accepted
A genuine but slow-developing effect could be abandoned at the deadline. Accepted
deliberately: the alternative failure mode has already been observed for two years.
