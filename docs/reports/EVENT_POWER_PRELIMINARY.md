## Read this with

- **POWERED is not a finding.** It means a registered study could see 0.5% a month if it were there; it says nothing about whether it is.
- **A powered population on a short series is still short.** Announcements start in 2023 and results filings in 2024 in this archive: few monthly cohorts, so the serial correction is estimated on little data and one bad year moves it.
- **Holder entries and exits are filing-to-filing changes in who is NAMED above 1%.** Crossing the 1% line is not the same as buying or selling, and a filing published late is dated late.
- **Next step for any powered row:** a written design and the owner's registration, before any mean is computed.
